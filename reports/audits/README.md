# Audits

Adversarial audit reports for AXOS. Audits are independent checks
against the implementation; they do not modify it.

## Contents

This directory is the home for audit-suite evidence (e.g. the R14 audit
suite under `src/axos/audit/`). Per-audit reports are added here as
they are produced.

*No audit reports are currently filed in this directory; the R14 audit
suite and its evidence live under `src/axos/audit/` and
`reports/historical/` respectively.*
